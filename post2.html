TrixOS v1.0.2 Released

August 11, 2026

We're happy to announce the release of TrixOS v1.0.2 – a maintenance update that brings several improvements and new features.

What's New

  • Renamed to GUI – The standalone version is now called TrixOS_Gui to better reflect its purpose as a full-featured graphical interface.
  • Tooltips for Tweaks – Hover over any tweak to see a detailed description of what it does and who it's recommended for.
  • Tweak Status Detection – When you open the app, already applied tweaks are automatically checked. No more guessing which tweaks are active.
  • History Tab – All applied and reverted tweaks are now logged with timestamps. You can track exactly what changes were made and when.
  • Show Commands Button – Click the 👁️ button next to any tweak to see the exact registry or PowerShell commands that will be executed.
  • Updated About Section – New links to the official website, YouTube channel, and email address.
  • Installer Component Selection – The installer now lets you choose which components to install (GUI, CLI, Cloud) during setup.
  • Improved Update Check – Uses cache-busting to always fetch the latest version and proper version comparison (now handles 1.0.10 correctly).
